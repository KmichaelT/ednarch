<TextBlock title="Overview">
A two-story commercial building turned into a destination cafe, with floor-to-ceiling glazing on two facades and a double-height void connecting the ground floor to a mezzanine dining level.

The plans are fully dimensioned on a structural grid (A–C, 1–3), with room names, finishes, and floor areas labeled on the sheet.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="From plan to building">
Two glazed facades, framed by slender white piers, make the interior the building's main feature. The yellow steel stair rises through the void, and the solid back walls hold the kitchen and service spaces.
</TextBlock>
