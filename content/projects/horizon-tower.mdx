<TextBlock title="Overview">
Horizon Tower stacks three uses in one structure: retail at the street, commercial floors in the podium, and residences in the tower, over three levels of basement parking.

The drawing set below covers the building at 1:100, from the site plan to a full-height section. Structural gridlines, floor levels, and room labels carry through every sheet so the plans, section, and elevations read as one coordinated set.
</TextBlock>
