<TextBlock title="Overview">
A three-story home for a family of five, with separate wings for parents and children, outdoor space on every level, and covered parking for three cars inside a walled compound.

Each floor plan is drawn on the same structural grid (A–E, 1–3), so walls, openings, and the stair line up from floor to floor.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="From plan to building">
Each floor steps back from the one below, so every main room opens onto a covered terrace and the upper floors shade the lower ones. The renderings show how the plans read as built form: white render on the main volumes, natural stone at the entry, and dark panels on the private zones.
</TextBlock>
